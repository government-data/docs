**How to read it.** The band says how far each customer's record can be
relied on. Companies usually come out
worse partly because of fields their record does not carry: that is a problem
of the process, not of the companies, and it is fixed at the source.

<Chart id="recency" />

**How to read it.** Data may be right and still be old. Everything not updated
within the year weighs down component A of the data reliability index (ICD).

<Chart id="top-fields" />

**How to read it.** A field far ahead of the rest usually has a single cause
(an update date nobody refreshes, a catalog): it is fixed once, at the source
or with a campaign, rather than record by record.
