## Remediation queues

Each record failing a rule is a finding, and findings fall into one queue per
value of a grouping field (here, the office), each with its service level agreement (SLA). A finding is
overdue when it has stayed open longer than its queue's SLA. In the
product, **Overdue** reads 0 until findings are carried from one cut to the
next ([Reading the dashboard](../../guide/charts/#remediation-queues)).

<Chart id="queues" />

**How to read it.** Red is what to chase first. An office with many open
findings and few overdue is keeping up; one where most are overdue needs
attention before its queue grows.
