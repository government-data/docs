## p′ chart by office (level 2)

<Chart id="p-chart" />

**How to read it.** An office above the upper limit fails more than normal
variation explains: look for the cause at the point of capture (process,
advisor, form). One below the lower limit does something better, worth
copying. Points between the curves are noise: no action.

## Income outliers (level 3)

<Chart id="outliers" />

**How to read it.** Grey points are normal for their occupation. A point
beyond ±3.5 is an outlier, not an error by itself: review it, fix the errors
(extra digits, expenses far above income) and keep the legitimate values.

## Drift with PSI

<Chart id="psi" />

**How to read it.** The population stability index (PSI) compares this
cut's spread with the previous one's. The new batch has fewer customers in the low deciles and
more in the high ones. Each record may be valid and the batch still be
suspicious: with a moderate PSI, review it before using it.
