**Who does this:** anyone who follows the quality of a dataset. Every
ready-made policy can read the dashboard: `quality-viewer` and `auditor`
for managers and compliance readers, `quality-analyst` and
`quality-steward` for those who act on it. The dashboard shows totals,
never a record's values. This page is the order to read it in;
[Reading the dashboard](../charts/) explains each chart on its own.

## To open a dataset's dashboard

1. In the **Explorer** in the side bar, open **Lake**: each dataset is a
   line marked **◆**.
2. Open the dataset, **◆ customers**, and choose **Dashboard**.

**You should see** the page **Dashboard of customers**, with the **Cut**
selector at the top and the charts under it. The same charts are in the
**Quality** tab of the table the dataset reads, opened from **Sources** in
the Explorer.

## To choose the cut and the dataset

- **The dataset.** Each dataset has its own dashboard. To compare two
  datasets, open each one's; there is no dashboard across datasets.
- **The cut.** **Cut** lists the dataset's evaluated cuts, the latest
  first. Every chart shows the cut chosen, worked out at that cut's date,
  never today's, so an old cut keeps saying what it said on its day.
  Charts that compare (bands, groups that changed, drift) compare it with
  the evaluated cut before it.

To see what changed between two dates, read the trend first, then choose
each cut in turn and compare the tables under the charts. More in [Before
you read a chart](../charts/#before-you-read-a-chart).

## Follow a question down

A question about a dataset usually goes from the whole to the detail: is
it getting better, what kind of problem, where, which rule, which group.
Each step is one or two charts at the same cut.

## Is quality getting better or worse?

Read **Conformity and IGCD by cut**: the share of rule checks that passed,
and the IGCD (overall data quality index), the dataset's one figure, from 0
to 1, over the last twelve cuts. Rising is good. The axis does not start at
zero, so read the values in the table under it before calling a step a
change; a jump together with a jump in records usually means a different
population, not better data. More in [Conformity and IGCD by
cut](../charts/#conformity-and-igcd-by-cut).

## What kind of problem dominates?

Read **Failures by dimension and severity**: completeness, validity,
timeliness and the other dimensions, split into blocking, major and minor.
Start with blocking and major. Many completeness failures point at how the
data is captured; many timeliness failures at data nobody has updated.
More in [Failures by dimension and
severity](../charts/#failures-by-dimension-and-severity).

## Where does it fail?

Read **Fields with the most failures**, then **Fields with the lowest ICD**
(data reliability index). A
field with many failures may simply be the most checked: its table says
how many rules read it. A field low in ICD but with few failures is
usually old rather than wrong. **Emptiest fields** shows which fields are
missing most, as shapes, never values. More in [Fields with the most
failures](../charts/#fields-with-the-most-failures) and [Fields with the
lowest ICD](../charts/#fields-with-the-lowest-icd).

## Which rule fails, and on what?

1. Read **Rules with the lowest conformity**: each rule with its field,
   dimension, severity, the records it applies to and its failures. A rule
   that applies to few records swings a lot from cut to cut.
2. To see what the rule checks, open it in the Explorer, under its field
   (**◆ customers**, then `Email`, then `EML-02`): **As read** says it in
   words.
3. To see examples, choose **Test**. The **Result** tab shows the rule's
   counts and a few records of each outcome. It runs on the dataset's
   latest cut, whatever cut the dashboard shows.

Values in **Result** come masked; if your policy lets you see personal
data, as `quality-analyst` does, **Show values** shows them with a reason:
see [Personal data and the audit
trail](../personal-data-and-audit/). Low conformity means the data is wrong
or the rule is badly posed: check the rule with its author before anyone
fixes thousands of records. More in [Rules with the lowest
conformity](../charts/#rules-with-the-lowest-conformity).

## Which group fails?

When the dataset has a grouping field, such as `Region`, the charts by
group are drawn once for each grouping field:

- **p′ chart by group**: which groups fail more than normal, and which
  only look worse by chance. Only dots outside the curves are signals. See
  [p′ chart by group](../charts/#p-chart-by-group).
- **Largest groups** and **Groups that changed most**: where most records
  are, and which groups moved since the cut before.
- **Remediation queues**: how much is waiting in each group. Working a
  queue is in [Work a remediation queue](../work-a-queue/).

## What the dashboard does not answer yet

- Which records make up a figure: no chart opens the records behind it.
  **Test** on a rule gives examples from the latest cut.
- A chart that needs a field role the mapping does not give, such as a
  grouping field or an update date: there is no screen yet to change the
  mapping.
- A copy to take away: charts cannot be exported or downloaded yet.

## Problems

| What you see                                              | What it means and what to do                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **No cut of this dataset has been evaluated yet.**        | Nothing to chart yet. Ask your data steward to run a cut ([Run and schedule cuts](../run-cuts/)).                                 |
| A chart's name followed by **no data for this cut**       | The chart applies to the dataset but this cut gave it nothing, such as a first cut with nothing to compare with. It is not zero.  |
| A chart you expected is not there                         | The dataset's mapping lacks the field it needs: see [Before you read a chart](../charts/#before-you-read-a-chart).                |
| **Could not load. Try again.**                            | Reload the page. If it stays, ask your administrator whether your policy lets you view results for this dataset.                  |
| No **Explorer** in the side bar                           | Your policies let you see neither rules nor sources. Ask your administrator.                                                      |
| Your group is not in **Remediation queues**               | The chart shows the fifteen groups with the most open findings: see [Work a remediation queue](../work-a-queue/#what-a-queue-is). |
| **Rules with the lowest conformity** shows "—" for a rule | The rule applied to no record at this cut.                                                                                        |
