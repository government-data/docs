**For whom:** administrators and technical reviewers who want to see the
[architecture](../overview/#architecture) at work.

How an installation behaves, played out: its machines grouped by module,
the traffic between them, the quality flow of a dataset and a third-party
batch stage by stage, a big-data job on Trino, an update pulled from AWS,
and what happens, step by step, when something fails. Pick a scenario;
pause it, change its speed, go step by step, or start another.

<PlatformSimulation />

## The scenarios

- **Quality flow:** the core compiles the approved rules on the catalog's
  main line into SQL and signs it; a worker of the
  [orchestrator](../orchestrator/) runs it on [Trino](../trino/), which
  reads the source into bronze, derives silver, evaluates the rules and
  writes the results to gold. See [Apache Iceberg](../iceberg/) for the
  three layers and [Formulas](../../formulas/) for the indicators.
- **Third-party batch:** a provider's batch through five gates, from
  quarantine to the owner's approval and a load that can be undone.
- **Normal run** and **big-data job:** every part doing its own job, and
  Trino spreading a very large job over its workers, with one added.
- **Update from AWS:** the [agent](../agent/) pulls a signed version,
  backs up and applies it in steps. See [Updates from AWS](../aws/).
- **Failures:** a worker of the orchestrator, a source, a worker of Trino,
  a server of the lake, and an update that does not come up.

## How to read it

The lines carry the kind of traffic, as in the architecture diagram, and
each packet names what it carries. A machine's state is in its border and
its badge: ⟳ working, ✕ down, ⚙ being updated, + just added; a dashed
machine can be added. The stages of a flow show ✓ passed, ! warned or ✕
failed, with their result. While a scenario plays, Space pauses it and the
right arrow goes to the next step.

:::note
It is an illustration of the design, not a measurement: the times and
figures in the log are the story's, invented for it. Some parts are still
being rolled out; see [Implementation
status](../overview/#implementation-status).
:::
