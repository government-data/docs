**For whom:** administrators and technical reviewers who want to see the
[architecture](../overview/#architecture) at work.

An installation's machines, drawn as a room: the main server, a Trino
worker and the lake server, with people's desk, the organisation's source
databases, a third-party provider, and AWS, which the agent pulls updates
from. The tour goes through the stack one technology at a time, and lights
every unit and cable where it takes part.

<StackFigure name="stack-sim" wide />

## How to read it

- **The tour** moves on by itself. Any click, key or pointer over the text
  pauses it on its stop; **▶** or **Space** resumes it. The arrow keys, or a
  click on the figure, go to the next or the previous technology, and the
  list under the figure goes to any of them.
- **A lit unit** is a place where the technology runs. A unit **outlined in
  amber and marked optional** runs only on a machine an installation adds:
  a second Trino worker, or the lake replica at another site.
- **A cable** stands for every connection between its two machines; its
  arrow points from the side that opens the connection. Nothing connects
  into the installation from outside: the agent opens the line to AWS.
- **Point at a row** under the figure to light only that row's places.
- **The cards on the machines' tops** give disk, CPU and memory. The one in
  red is the resource that machine needs most.

:::note
It is an illustration of the design, not a measurement. The sizes marked \*
are proposed starting points to size a first installation by; size a real
one from its data. Some parts are still being rolled out; see
[Implementation status](../overview/#implementation-status).
:::
