## Before you start

- A Linux server (Ubuntu, Debian, Red Hat Enterprise Linux or a derivative)
  with Docker and its Compose plugin, ports 80 and 443 free, and access as
  root (`sudo`) for whoever installs it.
- Access from the server to the internet over HTTPS, the secure web
  protocol: the server downloads its versions and updates from there.
  Nothing on the internet connects to the server.
- The name people will type to reach the product, such as
  `quality.example.org`, pointing to the server on your network.
- Your organisation's mail server (SMTP, the standard mail protocol, or
  Office 365), to send colleagues their activation links.

## 1. Install

Whoever supports your installation runs the Quality Data installer on the
server, as root. It checks the server, registers it with its certificate
(the only way the server proves who it is when it downloads a version),
downloads the version to install, checks its signature, generates the
installation's passwords, starts the product and makes it start with the
server. Running it again does only what is missing and keeps the data and
the passwords.

**You should see**, from them, the address to open and a link to choose
your password as the first administrator, with your e-mail. The link works
once. If the browser warns about the site's certificate, ask them which
certificate to trust on the computers that will open the product.

## 2. Sign in for the first time

Open the link. On **Activate your account**, type your password in **New
password** and **Repeat the new password**, following the rules listed, and
choose **Activate the account**. On **Sign in**, type your **Email** and
**Password** and choose **Sign in**.

**You should see** the product open, signed in as you. If the link expired,
ask whoever supports your installation for a new one: they make it on the
server.

## 3. Set up outgoing mail

Accounts are activated by e-mail, so mail comes first. In the menu at the
top right, choose **Settings**, then **Outgoing mail**. If the card says
**Not configured**, turn on **Send mail**, pick the **Mail provider**, fill
in the fields (each has its help), use **Send test**, then **Save**.

**You should see** the card on **Settings** say _On_, and the test message
in your inbox.

## 4. Create a colleague's account

In **Settings**, open **Users and access**, tab **Users**, and choose
**Register user**. In **New user**, type their **Email**, optionally their
name, leave **Role** as **User**, and choose **Create and send the link**.

**You should see** the account in the list, **Waiting for activation**, with
**Nothing** under **Access**. The role **Administrator** can do everything,
without policies.

## 5. Give them a policy

On the account's row, choose **Edit**. Under **Policies attached
directly**, tick the ready-made policy for their work, and choose **Save
changes**:

| They will…                             | Policy            |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Connect sources, write rules, run cuts | `quality-steward` |
| Work a remediation queue               | `quality-analyst` |
| Read dashboards and indicators         | `quality-viewer`  |
| Read dashboards and the audit trail    | `auditor`         |

**You should see** the policy under **Access**. To give the same policies to
several people at once, use the **Groups** tab.

## 6. Your colleague signs in

They open the link in their e-mail, choose their password on **Activate
your account**, and sign in. **You should see** their account become
**Active**. If the e-mail is lost or the link expired, use **Resend link**.

## Before anyone measures data

The installer sets up the application: the web screens, sign-in, settings,
updates and its own database. Mapping tables and taking cuts also need the lake, the
rules catalog and at least one worker, which run on their own and which the
product cannot install by itself yet. If the **Explorer** in the side bar
stays empty and says **Could not load. Try again.**, ask your provider to set
them up before the data steward starts.
