**For** the person who gets failing data fixed, for an office, a region or a
channel. **At the end** you know where your group's queue is, how much is
open in it, which fields and rules make it up, and how to see a fix count.
The example is the `customers` dataset of [Concepts](../concepts/), whose
queues are split by `Region`.

## Before you start

- An account with the `quality-analyst` policy.
- A dataset with at least one evaluated cut and a grouping field, from your
  data steward. Without a grouping field a dataset has no queues.

## 1. Open the dashboard

In the **Explorer** in the side bar, open **Lake**, then the dataset,
**◆ customers**, and choose **Dashboard**.

**You should see** the page **Dashboard of customers**, with the **Cut**
selector at the top on the latest evaluated cut. If it says that no cut has
been evaluated yet, ask your data steward to run one.

## 2. Find your queue

Scroll to **Remediation queues**: one chart per grouping field, such as
**Remediation queues · Region**, with a column per group. Its table has,
for each group:

- **Open**: the findings waiting, each a record failing a rule at this cut.
- **Priority**: the open findings' summed priority; high with few findings
  means a few costly ones.
- **SLA** (service level agreement): the days the queue has to fix a
  finding, 30 unless your data
  steward set another under **Remediation SLAs**, below the charts.
- **Overdue**, **Resolved at this cut** and **Oldest open**: see below what
  they show today.

More in [Reading the dashboard: remediation
queues](../charts/#remediation-queues).

## 3. See what fails

On the same dashboard, **Fields with the most failures** and **Rules with
the lowest conformity** say which fields and rules produce the findings.
For examples of failing records, open the rule in the Explorer, under its
field, and choose **Test**: the **Result** tab shows its counts at the
latest cut and a few records of each outcome. They are records of the whole
dataset, not only of your group, masked. Your policy lets you show their
values whole with **Show values** and a purpose, and every view is
recorded in the audit trail.

## 4. Get it fixed at the source

Quality Data only reads. The records are corrected where they live, in the
source system, by whoever captures or owns them; tell them which rule
fails and why, as **As read** says it.

## 5. Check the fix

A finding is resolved when a later cut no longer fails it. Wait for the next
scheduled cut, or under the dataset's **Cuts** choose **Run now** (with
**Replace it (unless running)** if today's cut is there). Once it is
**Evaluated**, pick it in **Cut**: your group's **Open** should drop.

## What is not there yet

The product does not yet list the records of a queue, nor carry a finding
from one cut to the next. So today a queue shows counts only, and
**Overdue**, **Resolved at this cut** and **Oldest open** read 0, because
every open finding counts from the cut shown. Use **Open**, its change from cut to cut, and the
examples from **Test**.
