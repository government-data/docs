**For** managers and compliance readers, who follow the quality of the data
without touching it. **At the end** you have read a dataset's overall figure
at a cut, whether it is getting better, and where its failures are. The
example is the `customers` dataset of [Concepts](../concepts/).

## Before you start

- An account with the `quality-viewer` policy, or `auditor`, which also
  reads the audit trail in Grafana, the installation's monitoring
  application. Neither shows a record's values: the dashboard shows
  totals only.
- A dataset with at least one evaluated cut, from your data steward.

## 1. Open the dashboard

In the **Explorer** in the side bar, open **Lake**: each dataset is a line
marked **◆**. Open **◆ customers** and choose **Dashboard**.

**You should see** the page **Dashboard of customers**. If it says that no
cut has been evaluated yet, ask your data steward to run one.

## 2. Pick a cut

The **Cut** selector at the top lists the evaluated cuts, the latest first.
Every chart shows the cut you choose, and figures are always worked out at
that cut's date, so an old cut keeps saying what it said on its day.

**You should see** the charts redrawn for that date. A chart appears only
when the dataset gives it what it needs, so two datasets may show different
charts. More in [Reading the dashboard: before you read a
chart](../charts/#before-you-read-a-chart).

## 3. Read the trend

Start with **Conformity and IGCD by cut**: a line for the share of rule
checks that passed, and one for the IGCD (overall data quality index), the
dataset's one figure to follow over time, from 0 to 1. Rising is good. Point
at a dot to see its value; the table under the chart has every cut. The axis
does not start at zero, so check the table before calling a step a change.
More in [Conformity and IGCD by
cut](../charts/#conformity-and-igcd-by-cut).

## 4. See where it fails

Then read, at the same cut:

- [Failures by dimension and
  severity](../charts/#failures-by-dimension-and-severity): which kind of
  quality fails (completeness, validity, timeliness…) and how seriously.
- [Fields with the most failures](../charts/#fields-with-the-most-failures):
  where to look first.
- [Remediation queues](../charts/#remediation-queues), when the dataset
  has a grouping field: how much is waiting in each group.
