**Who does this:** an analyst, with the `quality-analyst` policy, works a
queue: reads it, looks at failing records with their values, and runs a
cut to check a fix. A data steward, with `quality-steward`, sets each
queue's SLA. The first time through is in [Getting started:
analyst](../start-analyst/); this page is the fuller account.

## What a queue is

A **finding** is a record failing a rule at a cut: a customer failing two
rules is two findings. A **remediation queue** gathers the findings of one
value of a grouping field: with `Region` as the grouping field, North is
one queue and South another, and records with no region make the queue
**(empty)**. A dataset with two grouping fields has two sets of queues, and
each finding is in one queue of each.

Each queue has a service level agreement (SLA): the days it has to fix a
finding before the finding is overdue, 30 unless a steward sets another.

A dataset has queues only when its mapping has a grouping field and a
record key; mapping a table proposes both. The dashboard shows, for each
grouping field, the fifteen groups with the most open findings, in the
chart and in its table alike: a group with fewer is not listed.

## To read your queue

1. Open the dataset's **Dashboard** in the Explorer (**◆ customers**,
   **Dashboard**) and keep the latest **Cut**.
2. Scroll to **Remediation queues · Region**: a column of open findings
   per group, and the table under it.
3. Find your group's row:
   - **Open**: its findings at this cut.
   - **Priority**: the open findings' priority added up. High with few
     findings means a few costly ones, in important fields with a low data reliability index (ICD)
     ([Formulas §15](../formulas/#15-priority-and-remediation-queues)).
   - **SLA**: the queue's days.
   - **Overdue**, **Resolved at this cut** and **Oldest open**: they read 0
     today. The product does not yet carry a finding from one cut to the
     next, so every open finding counts as opened at the cut shown: none
     is old enough to be overdue, and none is seen to close.

So today the figure to work with is **Open**, and how it changes from one
cut to the next.

## To set the remediation SLAs

A data steward does this; the form shows only to policies that may edit
the dataset's pipeline, as `quality-steward` does.

1. On the dataset's **Dashboard**, below the charts, open **Remediation
   SLAs**. Its note calls the grouping field a "dimension": each box is
   one grouping field, named after it (`Region`).
2. In **Every group (days)**, type the days for every group of that field,
   from 1 to 365. Leave it empty to keep 30.
3. For a group that needs other days, choose **Add a group** and type the
   **Group** exactly as the chart shows it, such as `North`, and its
   **Days**. Leave **Group** empty for the records with no value. **×**
   removes the line.
4. Choose **Save**.

**You should see** _SLAs saved: the queues show them._ and the queues'
chart redrawn with the new **SLA**. No new evaluation is needed. The form
saves every line at once: a line you removed goes back to the field's
days. Each save is recorded in the audit trail.

## To find what fails

The dashboard says what fails in the whole dataset; nothing yet narrows it
to one group's records.

1. On the same cut, read **Fields with the most failures** and **Rules
   with the lowest conformity**: the fields and rules behind most
   findings. **Largest groups** gives your group's share of records with a
   serious failure; **p′ chart by group** says whether that share is
   unusual or chance ([p′ chart by group](../charts/#p-chart-by-group)).
2. Open a failing rule in the Explorer, under its field, and read **As
   read**: what the rule asks, in words.
3. Choose **Test**. The **Result** tab shows the rule's counts at the
   dataset's latest cut and up to 10 records of each outcome, from the
   whole dataset, not only your group, masked. With `quality-analyst`,
   **Show values** shows them whole with a purpose (such as **Remediation
   of data**), and each view is recorded in the audit trail.

To look at a record as it is now in the source, open the table under
**Sources** and choose **Records** ([Connect a
source](../connect-a-source/#to-look-at-a-tables-records)).

## To get it fixed in the source

Quality Data only reads: no record is fixed inside it. The records are
corrected where they live, in the source system, by whoever captures or
owns them. Tell them, for each rule: its id, what **As read** says, the
field, and the example records **Test** showed, by their record key. Keep
values out of e-mails and tickets where your organisation's rules ask you
to.

## To confirm at the next cut

A fix counts when a later cut no longer fails the record.

1. Wait for the dataset's next scheduled cut, or take one now: under the
   dataset's **Cuts**, choose **Run now**, with **If today's cut is there**
   on **Replace it (unless running)** when today's cut exists ([Run and
   schedule cuts](../run-cuts/#to-run-a-cut-now)).
2. Reload the page until the cut is **Evaluated**, then open the
   **Dashboard** and choose it in **Cut**.
3. Compare your group's **Open** with the cut before: choose each in
   **Cut** in turn.

**You should see** **Open** drop by the findings fixed. What still fails
stays open: go round the cycle again with what remains.

## What is not there yet

- **A queue's records.** A queue shows counts; it does not list its
  findings or their records. Use **Test** for examples.
- **Findings carried from cut to cut.** Until they are, **Overdue**,
  **Resolved at this cut** and **Oldest open** read 0.
- **Assigning or closing by hand.** A finding cannot be given to a person
  or closed from a screen: the next cut is the proof.
- **Warnings.** Nothing tells you when a queue passes its SLA.

## Refusals and messages

| What you see                                                                                | What it means and what to do                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No **Remediation queues** chart                                                             | The dataset has no grouping field. Ask your data steward.                                                 |
| Your group is not in the chart                                                              | It is not among the fifteen with the most open findings at this cut.                                      |
| No **Remediation SLAs** below the charts                                                    | Your policy may not edit the dataset's pipeline, or there are no queues.                                  |
| **The dataset's mapping has no field with the dimension role: its findings have no queue.** | The dataset has no grouping field, so there is nothing to set.                                            |
| **Days are a whole number from 1 to 365.**                                                  | Fix the days on the line marked and save again.                                                           |
| **A group is set twice in the same dimension.**                                             | Two lines name the same group under one grouping field: remove one.                                       |
| **A group's value is too long.**                                                            | A group's name is longer than any value can be: check what you typed.                                     |
| **A dimension is no longer in the dataset's mapping: reload the page.**                     | The mapping changed since you opened the page. Reload it and set the SLAs again.                          |
| **The dataset has no mapping on main.**                                                     | The dataset is not on `main` any more. Ask your data steward.                                             |
| **Too many SLAs for one dataset.**                                                          | More group lines than a dataset can hold: keep the groups that really differ.                             |
| **You do not have permission for this.**                                                    | Your policy may not change the SLAs.                                                                      |
| **The catalog does not answer: it cannot be checked now.**                                  | The store of mappings and rules is not responding. Try again later; if it lasts, tell your administrator. |
